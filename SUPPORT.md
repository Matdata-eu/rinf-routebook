# Getting help

Routebook is a community project; help happens in the open on GitHub, so that the answer helps the next person too.

- **How do I ...?** Read the [README](README.md), [deployment guide](docs/deployment.md) and [architecture notes](docs/architecture.md) first. Then ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Matdata-eu/rinf-routebook/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/Matdata-eu/rinf-routebook/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include the dataset, the route (the URL of the app holds it) and what you expected.
- **An idea or a feature request:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/Matdata-eu/rinf-routebook/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Wrong or missing data:** Routebook shows RINF as published by ERA and the infrastructure managers. If the data itself is wrong, the infrastructure manager can correct it in RINF. If Routebook reads it wrongly, open a bug report.
- **A security problem:** don't open an issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

For professional support, custom extensions or deployments for your organisation, contact [Matdata](https://matdata.eu).
