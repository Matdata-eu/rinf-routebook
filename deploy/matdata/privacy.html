Privacy statement

RINF Routebook, public demo at routebook.matdata.eu. Last updated: 18 October 2026.

Who is responsible

This website is published and hosted by Matdata, the organisation behind the open-source RINF Routebook project. Matdata is the controller for the personal data described below. For questions about this statement or your data, use the contact details on matdata.eu, or open an issue on GitHub (issues are public: do not put personal data in them).

Server logs

The website runs on a server operated by Matdata. When you open a page, the server receives the technical data that every web request carries: your IP address, the date and time, the requested page and your browser's user agent. This data is kept in the server's access logs for a short time, to deliver the website, to keep it secure and to find faults, and is then deleted. It is not combined with other data and not passed on to anyone.

Map tiles and fonts

To draw the map, your browser loads images and fonts from these third parties, which therefore receive your IP address and the usual request data:

  • OpenStreetMap Foundation (tile.openstreetmap.org), for the street map: privacy policy;
  • MapLibre (demotiles.maplibre.org, hosted on GitHub Pages), for the map label fonts.

The aerial imagery (Esri World Imagery) is different: your browser asks our server for it, and our server fetches the tiles from Esri (ibasemaps-api.arcgis.com) with its own address. Esri does not receive your IP address, only the server's requests and the map areas looked at. See Esri's privacy statement.

The links to RINF Atlas in the Datasets dialog open another website published by Matdata, with its own privacy statement.

What this website does not do

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • No accounts and no forms: everything you do (choosing operational points, routes, filters) happens in your browser and is not sent to Matdata, apart from the requests for pages, data and map tiles described above.
  • Your display settings (which item types you hide) and whether you have read the demo notice are kept in your browser's local storage, on your own device. You can clear them in your browser at any time.

Railway data

The railway data shown is public data from the Register of Infrastructure (RINF) of the European Union Agency for Railways, fetched by the server and kept current. It describes infrastructure, not people.

Legal basis and retention

The processing of the technical request data is needed to deliver and secure the website, which is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The server logs are deleted after a short time; how long the map providers keep their logs is set out in their own privacy statements.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To use these rights, contact Matdata as described above; for the logs kept by the map providers, you can also contact them directly. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

Changes

We may update this statement when the website changes. The current version is always on this page, and its history is in the project repository.
